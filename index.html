    Need answers

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does UC delivery take?

    Usually 5–15 minutes after your JazzCash payment has been manually verified by the store owner. During busy hours it can take longer.

    Is my payment automatically verified?

    No. This store uses manual verification. Uploading a receipt or clicking a button does not mark your order as paid. The owner checks the real JazzCash transaction first, and only then is your order marked Verified.

    What do I need to place an order?

    Your PUBG Mobile Player ID, your in-game name, a JazzCash account for payment, and a screenshot or PDF of the payment receipt.

    What happens if I enter the wrong Player ID?

    UC is sent to whatever Player ID you provide. Always copy it directly from your PUBG profile. The store cannot recover UC sent to an incorrect ID.

    How do I know my order is confirmed?

    Your order starts as Pending, becomes Verified after the owner confirms the payment, and then UC is delivered. You can check any time with your Order ID in the Track Order section.

    Can I get a refund?

    Because UC is a digital in-game currency, purchases are final once delivered. Refunds are only considered when the store fails to deliver the purchased UC.
